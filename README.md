# Mathematical Research Portfolio

A selection of my undergraduate mathematical research reports. My academic interests include probability theory, random matrix theory, mathematical analysis, and computational approaches to understanding complex systems.

I enjoy the process of constructing rigorous mathematical arguments: starting from assumptions, exploring possible approaches, and examining whether each step is justified. My projects have given me experience reading research literature, working independently on theoretical problems, and connecting mathematical reasoning with numerical investigation.
